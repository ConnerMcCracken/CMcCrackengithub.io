Customer Review

"Crack & Coles Ice Cream Shop is hands down one of the best dessert spots I’ve ever visited. From the moment you walk in, you’re greeted by the rich, sweet aroma of freshly made waffle cones and a cheerful, welcoming vibe. Their flavors are incredibly creative—think brown butter caramel swirl, blueberry cheesecake, and a chocolate espresso that actually packs a punch. Everything tastes homemade, creamy, and perfectly balanced, not overly sweet. The staff are friendly and generous with samples, which makes choosing (almost) easier. Whether you’re stopping in for a quick scoop or hanging out with friends, Crack & Coles makes it feel like a special treat every time. Definitely a must-visit if you love ice cream." -Anonymous Review
